# Enicar 101: models and generations Enicar World has no record for

Checked against the names, nicknames and references in data/references/*.yaml on 2026-10-27.

## Models and references

- **Ref. 817 CH cockpit chronograph** (dashboard clock, not a wristwatch).
  https://enicar101.com/2020/12/07/the-jet-age-enicar-ch817/
  Ref. 817 CH, 75 mm, black case and dial, 15-minute counter, red internal lighting, crown-pusher at 12; in the 1960 catalogue; Enicar adverts of 1961 and 1964 claim it supplied DC-8 and Caravelle cockpits. No calibre given (the caption on one photo names a Minerva 19/9CH). Linked for now from sherpa-guide MkI (the adverts show the early Guide).
- **"Garnix" chronograph, ref. 2303 / 072/003** (unnamed dial, entry-level chronograph).
  https://enicar101.com/2019/11/03/the-pre-sherpa-mystery/
  Gives refs 2303 and 072/003 and argues it is not a "pre-Sherpa". Only mentioned on our side in the ocean-pearl barometer label; linked from ocean-pearl.
- **Ultrasonic Sherpas Chronometer, ref. 100/76 AaNS** (diver in the Seapearl/Healthways style case).
  https://enicar101.com/2021/01/20/enicar-chronometers-at-the-cold-edge-of-the-world/
  Ref. 100/76 AaNS, automatic cal. AR 1134 with chronometer certificate; tied to the 1957/58 Swiss International Geophysical Year arctic expedition (Spitsbergen). Linked from healthways.
- **Sherpa Dive refs. 100/124 BaANXS and 100/224 BaANXS.**
  https://enicar101.com/2024/01/14/hans-hass-expedition-xarifa-ii-and-the-enicar-sherpa-dive/ (100/124 BaANXS, bakelite bezel, Hans Hass 1957)
  https://enicar101.com/2021/02/20/swedens-apollo-program-the-salvage-of-the-kings-ship-vasa-in-1961/ (100/224 BaANXS, aluminium bezel, no red ring)
  Our sherpa-dive record lists only 100/244; worth checking whether 100/224 and 100/244 are the same number misread on one side.
- **Sherpa Graph Cousins** (non-Enicar look-alikes, not a model): https://enicar101.com/sherpa-graph-cousins/ - linked from sherpa-graph as a buyer's guide only.

## Generations our records lack

- **Sherpa Super Graph** has no `variants`; Enicar 101 describes four:
  - MK I, ref. 072/002, 1963, serials 626.6xx, 627.0xx-627.1xx, 635.8xx, 706.8xx, under 200 made (est.): https://enicar101.com/super-graph-coming-soon/
  - "Simonet", ref. 072/002, made for the 1964 Tokyo Olympic shooting events, serials 627.0xx-627.1xx, under 50 (est.), black bezel with red segments: https://enicar101.com/super-graph-simonet/
  - MK II, ref. 072-02-02, from mid-1966, serial 906.7xx, about 100 (est.): https://enicar101.com/super-graph-mk-ii/
  - MK III, ref. 072-02-02, about 1969, serials 1.070.1xx and 1.321.9xx (within Aqua Graph batches): https://enicar101.com/super-graph-mk-iii/
  Our record's years (1963-1966) end before E101's MK III. The six photos in placements.json are untagged; if generations are added, they map as: super-graph-coming-soon_01 = MK I, super-graph-mk-ii_01..03 = MK II, super-graph-mk-iii_01 = MK III, super-graph-simonet_01 = Simonet.
- **Sherpa Graph MK II**: Enicar 101 splits it into MK IIa (072/001, 601.5xx-601.9xx and 617.1xx-617.5xx, about 1,000) and MK IIb (072/001, 246.1xx-246.6xx, about 600, lollipop chronograph hand on later ones, thin register hands). Our MkII covers both; both pages are already linked from MkII.
- **Sherpa Graph MkId**: E101's page has only reference, serial range and case data (561.7xx-562.1xx, 500 est.), no photos.
- **Jet Graph MkIb**: E101's page is empty; serial range 721.9xx and an estimate of about 100 appear in https://enicar101.com/2019/06/12/more-motorsport-and-numbers/.
